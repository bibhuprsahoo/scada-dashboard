“Fat vs dairy” = society reading minus the dairy's receipt test for the same milk.

Why analyser drift matters

 
  • A drifted analyser over- or under-reads fat for every farmer on it
  • Payment is made on that reading — the error is systematic, not random
  • Different analyser makes are tracked separately, not pooled
  • Each society's reading is compared against the dairy's receipt test
  • A society reading 0.3% above its route peers is drifting, not lucky
